Rare and Exotic beans are sought after throughout the world, here are some you can try and find!

1500 year old Cave Bean

Found in a pot in Mexico, these beans have been carbon dated to be hundreds of years old. These beans are for collectors, with a unique nutty flavour.

Orca Beans

Named for their white and black colour, these bean are also known as Yin Yang beans, or Calypso Beans. These are perfect for any stew or salad, as they maintain their shape well, and double in size when cooked.

San Bernardo Blue

Named after the town where they originate, these beans may seem like normal green beans on the outside, but peeling the skin reveals a striking indigo blue.

Chicago Bean

This huge bean is also known as Cloud Gate. Located in Chicago, this bean isn't actually an edible bean, but a structure made out metal.
