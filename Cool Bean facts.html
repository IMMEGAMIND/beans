Tallest Bean

This stalk was suprisingly homegrown, reaching a massive height of 46 feet and 3 inches! That's about 3 adult giraffes stacked on top of each other, and is around half the height of the white house. You might need Jack to climb this one!

Bean Soup

Bean Soup

The American senate restaurant menu had bean soup for every day every since the early 1900's. Some say that it was from a strong request from an Idaho senator, or when a Minnesota senator introduced a resolution to have the dish served every day regardless of weather.

Greek Beans?

Greek Beans?

In ancient Greece, to find a political lead, they would use beans! They had counted exactly 99 black beans and only 1 white bean. They would pick 100 candidates, and whoever got the white bean would get the role. Luckily, they only chose small political roles with this method.
